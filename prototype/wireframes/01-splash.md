- Static, no animation
- Generic feel
- No emotional connection

## Animation Sequence
1. Logo fades in (0-500ms)
2. App name types in letter by letter (500-1000ms)
3. Tagline fades in (1000-1200ms)
4. Transition to next screen (1500ms)

## Design Specs

### Background
- Gradient: #FDF8F5 → #F8E8EC (warm white to soft rose)

### Logo
- Moon icon or custom illustration
- Size: 80dp x 80dp
- Soft shadow

### Typography
- App name: 36sp, Lexend Bold, #5B7DB1
- Tagline: 16sp, Lexend Regular, #3D4852
- Credit: 12sp, Lexend Light, #8A9099

### Loading Indicator
- Subtle horizontal progress bar
- Color: #E8A5B8 (soft rose)
- Or gentle pulse animation on logo
